### Round 1 — Day 1 (Tuesday, October 20, 2026)

*“It is quite humbling to start fresh. It takes a lot of courage. But it can be reinvigorating. You just have to put your ego on a shelf and tell it to be quiet.”* — Jennifer Ritchie Payette

Today, I get to “forget” the old and start anew, leaving behind old mindsets, patterns, dead weight, and, most importantly, ego.

It takes courage to admit what you don't know and embrace the discomfort of being a beginner again. Perhaps there is something beautiful about starting from scratch — the opportunity to learn, unlearn, and rediscover yourself without the weight of who you used to be. It means accepting that you won't know everything, that progress may sometimes feel slow, and that there will be days when your confidence is challenged. But it also means giving yourself permission to grow.

Today marks a huge turning point for me. I am choosing to step into a world that, in many ways, feels unfamiliar and even intimidating, willing to learn the ropes one step at a time. I don't have to be extraordinary today; I just have to show up, learn something, and keep going.

This journey is about more than learning to code. It is about cultivating discipline, embracing humility, and becoming comfortable with being uncomfortable. It is about learning to trust the process, even when the results aren't immediately visible.

I am reminding myself that **baby steps are still steps**. I don't need to have everything figured out, nor do I need to compare my beginning to someone else's middle. I only need the courage to begin, the patience to learn, and the commitment to return tomorrow a little better than I am today.

**Day 1 of 100. A fresh start. An open mind. One step at a time.**
